Privacy Policy

How Reel Spark handles your information.

Overview

Reel Spark respects your privacy. This website is designed to collect as little information as possible. This policy explains what happens to the information you share when you use it.

Information You Provide

When you use the order form, you may enter your name, WhatsApp number, selected service, budget, project description, a reference file name and a preferred delivery date. This information is used only to build a WhatsApp message on your device.

The order form does not send your details to any Reel Spark server or database. Nothing is shared with us until you choose to send the prepared message in WhatsApp. Files selected in the form are not uploaded. If you wish to share a file, you attach it yourself in the WhatsApp chat.

Information Shared Through WhatsApp

When you message Reel Spark on WhatsApp, we receive the details you send, including your phone number and any content or files you share. We use this only to respond to your inquiry, prepare quotations, deliver the services you order, and provide support.

WhatsApp is operated by a third party, and its own terms and privacy policy apply to messages sent through it.

Customer Content

Names, photographs, logos and other materials you provide for a project are used only to deliver that project. Any other use, such as showing the finished work in our portfolio, will be done only with your permission.

Cookies, Analytics and Third-Party Services

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics tools. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser may contact Google servers to download them. Links to WhatsApp and Instagram take you to services operated by third parties, with their own privacy practices.

If analytics or other tracking tools are added in future, this policy will be updated.

Sharing and Retention

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to deliver your order or where required by law. We keep order-related information for as long as needed to provide the service, handle support and meet legal or accounting obligations.
